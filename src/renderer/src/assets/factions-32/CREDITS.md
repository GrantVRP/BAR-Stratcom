# Faction emblem credits

32-bit versions of the Beyond All Reason faction icons (`sidepics/armada.png`, `sidepics/cortex.png` in
the Beyond All Reason repository), made for BAR StratCom.

- `<faction>-64.png`: 64 × 64 (launcher, plan headers)
- `<faction>-32.png`: 32 × 32 (cards, small badges)

Made by `tools/pixelate-factions.ts` (download kept in the git-ignored `data/raw/factions/`).
