# Launch scene 3D credits

Made by `tools/build-launch-3d.ts` from Beyond All Reason's game files and the Greenest Fields map:

- `model-armfast.json`: Sprinter model by FireStorm and PtaQ
- `model-corpyro.json`: Fiend model by Beherith and PtaQ
- `model-armwin.json`, `model-corwin.json`: Armada and Cortex wind turbines
- `model-ad0_aleppo2_*.json`, `tex-tree.png`: Aleppo pines by Beherith and 0 A.D.
- `walk-armfast.json`, `walk-corpyro.json`: walk animations by Beherith
- `death-armfast.json`, `death-corpyro.json`: death scripts of the Sprinter and Fiend (the Sprinter's
  death animation by Beherith); our own player and piece physics
- `model-armfast_dead.json`, `model-cor2X2C.json`, `tex-arm-wreck.png`, `tex-cor-wreck.png`: Beyond All
  Reason wreck models and wreck textures
- `fx-library.json`: numbers of Beyond All Reason's explosion effects (genericunitexplosion, burnblack),
  drawn with our own particles
- `tex-cor.png`: Cortex unit texture by Beherith
- `tex-arm.png`: derived from the Armada unit texture by Cremuss (CC BY-SA 4.0); this derived texture
  is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 as well
- `tex-ground.png`: Greenest Fields map texture by IceXuick, with the walking track added
