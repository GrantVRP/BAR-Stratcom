# Unit art credits

These sprites are 32-bit pixel versions of the Beyond All Reason unit icons by **IceXuick and Floris**,
made for BAR StratCom.

The original unit icons are © IceXuick and Floris, licensed CC BY-NC-ND (`/unitpics/` in the Beyond All
Reason repository).

- `64/<unitId>.png`: 64 × 64 sprites (cards, dossier)
- `32/<unitId>.png`: 32 × 32 sprites (timelines, small lists)

Made by `tools/fetch-unitpics.ts` (download, kept in the git-ignored `data/raw/unitpics/`) and
`tools/pixelate-unitpics.ts` (conversion).
